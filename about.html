About Me

I'm Christopher Cho, a Computer Science student at UC Irvine focused on systems and backend development. I enjoy working on projects that expose how software behaves under the hood—whether that's writing compilers and interpreters, building backend systems, or developing research tooling that connects implementation with evaluation.

My recent work includes Survey Sage, a conversational AI project for structured survey scoring; a Prolog interpreter and execution visualizer; a Lisp-to-x86-64 compiler written in Go; and Yield, a reinforcement learning study of speed and safety tradeoffs. These projects have helped me deepen my understanding of language implementation, backend integration, evaluation, and system design.

I enjoy exploring new programming paradigms and studying how compilers and operating systems shape modern development. However, outside of code I enjoy playing the electric guitar and creating my own metal riffs. My long-term goal is to work in backend or systems engineering, where performance, maintainability, and clarity all intersect.
